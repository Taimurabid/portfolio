<CaseSection title="Overview">

KeyFlo is a keyboard app that lets people send crypto without leaving the app they're typing in.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="My role">

Designed the iOS experience and built the backend.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="The problem">

Sending crypto usually means switching to a wallet app, copying addresses and switching back. KeyFlo puts it in the keyboard.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What I did">

I designed the full flow, from first-time setup to sending and confirming a transaction, so it feels as simple as sending a message. On the backend I built the services for WalletConnect wallet connections, blockchain API calls, smart contract interactions and push notifications.
