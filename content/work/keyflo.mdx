<CaseSection title="Overview">

Keyflo is a mobile keyboard with an embedded crypto wallet, so people can send stablecoin payments without leaving the app they're typing in.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="My role">

I designed the end-to-end mobile experience, from first-time setup to sending and confirming a transaction, with a focus on making crypto transfers feel as simple as sending a message.

</CaseSection>
