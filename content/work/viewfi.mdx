<CaseSection title="Overview">

ViewFi is a reward-based video advertising platform. Brands publish video campaigns that reward viewers with tokens, discounts, contests or VIP access in exchange for engagement and opt-in data. It works with YouTube, Vimeo and Livepeer videos, or ViewFi's own ViewPlay player.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="My role">

I led product design across onboarding, campaign setup and the rewarded viewing experience.

</CaseSection>
