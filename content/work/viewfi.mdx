<CaseSection title="Overview">

ViewFi lets brands reward viewers with tokens, discounts and exclusive access in exchange for watching and sharing opt-in data.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="My role">

Lead Product Designer for two years, also working on the services behind the product.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="The problem">

Getting set up took creators too long before they could launch their first campaign.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What I did">

I ran user research and usability tests, then redesigned onboarding and the campaign setup flow. I built the design system and worked with engineers through handoff. On the backend I worked on campaign and reward services with Stripe payments, Privy wallet sign-in, token rewards through Ethers.js, and video delivery through processing APIs and a CDN.
