<CaseSection title="Overview">

FabricBloc is BlocLabs' enterprise blockchain platform for business clients.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="My role">

Product designer and full-stack engineer. I designed it, built the front end and built the backend.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="The problem">

Blockchain products are usually built for developers. FabricBloc needed to make sense to business buyers who aren't technical.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What I did">

I designed the information architecture and user flows around how buyers evaluate the product. I built the web app in React, Next.js and TypeScript, and the REST and GraphQL APIs in NestJS on PostgreSQL and Redis. Ethers.js and Solidity contracts connect it to Ethereum, and webhooks keep it in sync with outside services. Everything runs in Docker on AWS.
