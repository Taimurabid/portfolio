<CaseSection title="Overview">

BlocLabs is a product studio that builds blockchain and AI products for clients.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="My role">

Redesigned and rebuilt the website, and added AI features across its products.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="The problem">

The team had to wait on a developer every time they wanted to post a case study or update a service page.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What I did">

I reworked the site's structure and messaging, redesigned every page in Figma, and rebuilt it in Webflow with CMS collections, so the team now publishes on their own. I also integrated the OpenAI and Claude APIs and built MCP integrations to add AI features to BlocLabs products.
